01
वंशावली कसरी हेर्ने
वृक्ष shows parents above children by default. Choose तेर्सो to read across the screen. Use + on a card to unfold the next generation.
सूची is a compact, expandable outline that is especially useful on phones. The same people and connections appear in both views.
Select a name to open their profile. परिवार, विवरण and स्रोत tabs keep relationships, photographs and evidence together. Close the profile to return to the full tree.
Choose a branch from the menu, or return to मूल पुर्खा at any time. You can copy a branch link to open the same branch on another device.

        
02
अभिलेख र संशोधन
This edition follows all branches in the Excel sheet “मुसुका सन्तान.” Repeated ancestors are combined; people with the same name in different families remain separate. Spouses and explicitly mentioned descendants in notes are included.
Nepali spelling is preserved from Excel. Romanized labels help with searching and are not official English spellings. Blank names, missing gender markers and unclear entries remain marked. A dashed connector indicates an entry needing clarification; a solid line does not certify historical accuracy.
स्व. is shown only for a person marked Deceased. Unknown life status is not treated as Living. Dates remain blank when absent. Parent and spouse links follow the spreadsheet’s generation columns and marriage blocks. Narrative notes do not establish an unnamed second parent.
Use the request button on any person or the अनुरोध tab to propose additions. The maintainer reviews requests and updates the master before publishing a new version. Phone numbers are excluded from the public records.

        
03
तस्बिर र सम्झना
Portraits appear inside a person’s profile and as small thumbnails in the tree when available. The Add photo button saves a preview in your current browser.
For a photograph visible to everyone, put the image in the photos folder and set its existing photo path in data.js. The upload guide explains the steps.
Browser previews are separate from shared photographs and are not included in the family-data download.

        
04
डिजाइनका आधार
The ancestor-first structure, compact descendant outline, optional profiles and view controls were informed by official documentation from FamilySearch, Ancestry, MyHeritage and Gramps Web.
The layout uses a slim green navigation rail on laptops and a compact green menu on phones. No permanent photo sidebar takes space away from the branches.
Read the research comparison, source links and decisions in the accompanying design note.
