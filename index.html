THE FAMILY RECORD

Beginning with मुसु

The genealogy begins in the narrow columns on the left: मुसु → हरि प्रसाद → चक्रपाणी → रामेश्वर → प्रजापति. The user confirmed Musu and Hari Prasad as the starting link.

The later branches include Kamalapati and Maheshwar, then Motilal, Ratnakhar, Dhanapati, Dandapani and Khim Prasad. The small early branches, missing-person entries and unnamed daughters are also represented.

Generation numbers follow the paper’s layout. Original column labels च, ङ, घ, ग, ख, क, A, B, C and D remain in the individual records.

१, २, ३
Appear to number males within a sibling group.
i, ii, iii
Appear to number females within a sibling group. Daughters and their recorded children remain connected.
+
Connects spouses. Multiple spouse entries are kept separate.
स्व.
Prefix for a person marked Deceased. Other people remain Unknown until the family supplies their status.
पिता / विवाह
पिता in a spouse’s note is not automatically the lineage person’s father. विवाह introduces marriage details.

The markers do not establish combined sibling birth order. A blank descendant cell does not prove someone has no children. Dashed lines indicate an entry needing clarification.

Your editable Excel register

Edit names, status, parent IDs and notes in People. Update marriages in Couples. The workbook also includes the source transcription and original photograph. Permanent IDs keep the website and workbook connected.

Return the revised workbook when you want a new website release. This static website does not automatically read changes from Excel.
