EASIEST UPDATE - SMALL FIX ZIP
1. Download and extract Bhusal-Website-Fix-v4.1.zip.
2. Open the bhusal-family.github.io repository, in the same Code folder shown
   in your screenshot.
3. Choose Add file > Upload files.
4. Upload index.html, styles.css, and data.js from the extracted ZIP. Replace
   the existing files. Do not upload the ZIP or a containing folder.
5. Commit with a message such as “Fix PDF links and contact order”.
6. Wait for the GitHub Pages deployment in Actions to finish.
7. Refresh the website. Mac: Command+Shift+R. Windows: Ctrl+Shift+R.
8. Click पूर्ण वंशावली, then PDF खोल्नुहोस् or डाउनलोड.
